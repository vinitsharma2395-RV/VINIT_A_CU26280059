Simple Pasta Recipe

A quick and delicious meal for any day of the week.

Ingredients

200 g pasta

2 tablespoons olive oil

2 cloves garlic, chopped

1 cup tomato sauce

Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

Boil the pasta in salted water until tender.

Heat the olive oil and cook the garlic for one minute.

Add the tomato sauce, salt, and pepper.

Drain the pasta, mix it with the sauce, and serve hot.
